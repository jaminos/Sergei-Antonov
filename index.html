About

For 18 years I've helped teams turn big, high-stakes IT projects and programmes into something predictable. I've led delivery units of up to 200 people and portfolios of up to $10M, and have been maintaining target margin across them.

Here's what makes it work. Ideas are tested as hypotheses first, and only what holds up moves into delivery. From there I agree on the outcome upfront and manage the vision of the result, keeping it clear to everyone throughout the project. I keep scope, budget and risk in check and build a team that can deliver it.

I manage delivery across different contexts and methodologies: waterfall, Agile, SAFe or a mix. I've also been responsible for the technical block, engineering excellence (SDLC, CI/CD, code quality, testing, security, infrastructure reliability), so speed never comes at the cost of quality. Today I add hands-on AI expertise, so teams move faster and decisions get sharper.

What I do

The areas I own on every engagement, from people and budgets to engineering.

Leading people

I build teams and invest myself in helping people grow, so new leaders emerge from within. I grew one account team from 40 to 80 people.

Planning & delivery economics

I own P&L, utilisation, margin and cash flow. I set up cost and time tracking so the cost and timeline of every project are visible at any moment.

Scope, risk & recovery

Change control stops scope creep and protects budgets. I find bottlenecks through metrics and project health radars before they become crises.

Stakeholders & negotiations

I work directly with CEOs, founders and client executives. I negotiate contract terms that keep projects healthy: advances, milestones and acceptance criteria.

PMO & delivery processes

I've built Delivery Management Offices and delivery frameworks that scale, and I keep them pragmatic rather than dogmatic.

Engineering excellence

I set up the engineering system behind delivery: SDLC, CI/CD, code quality standards, test automation and DevSecOps practices. Monitoring, resilient infrastructure and metrics like lead time and throughput keep releases frequent and systems reliable. I also run technical due diligence.

AI-enabled delivery

I embed AI tools and agents into the development lifecycle, and I launched an AI initiative hub to test ideas quickly.
